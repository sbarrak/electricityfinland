Recipients WhatsApp / email

Every recipient in the list gets the scheduled messages: WhatsApp when the phone and key are filled in, email when the address is filled in. The tick box at the start of a row is only for tests: it selects who gets the test from Send WhatsApp / Send email. WhatsApp uses CallMeBot: each person sends “I allow callmebot to send me messages” to +34 644 51 95 23 from their phone and gets their own API key.

A message is sent every day at 14:00 and at the extra times under Recipients, to every recipient in the list. It has a summary, then the hourly prices. When the price is at or above the high limit or at or below the low limit (switched on above the chart), one highlighted line is added for each such period and those hours are marked in the list. Extra times before 14:00 cover the time until midnight, because the next day is not fixed yet.
