How do I get the file? Oomi: OmaOomi → Kulutus (Consumption) → pick the address and the period → Lataa (.xls), then import that file here as it is (the file does not say which address it is for, so check the address before downloading). Any company: log in to Oma Datahub (Fingrid's free service, bank ID; it has the meter readings for Oomi and every other Finnish electricity company) and download the consumption of the period you want as a CSV file, with hourly or 15-minute values. An Excel (.xlsx) or CSV export from another service works too. Importing the same period again replaces it. The cost uses the price of each hour or 15 minutes with your Costs settings and the Spot / Total cost and Incl. VAT choices at the top.

Extra times before 14:00 cover the time until midnight, because the next day is not fixed yet.
